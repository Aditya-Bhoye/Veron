Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

We collect only what is needed to provide the software and process your payment.

What we collect

  • Account details (name, email, phone) are used to create and support your account.
  • Your business data is stored on your own computer. On paid plans it is also backed up to secure cloud storage, scoped privately to your outlet.
  • Payments are processed by our payment provider. We do not store your card or bank details on our systems.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.

Signing in with Google

  • When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. That is all we ask for, and it is used only to identify your account.
  • We do not read your Gmail, Drive, Contacts or Calendar, and we never request access to them.
  • You can disconnect VERON at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Where your data lives

  • Your bills, stock, staff and customer records are written first to the computer in your shop.
  • On paid plans the same records are synced to cloud storage so a second device can see them. Every record is scoped to your outlet and is not visible to any other business.
  • Personal fields on staff and customer cards are encrypted on your own machine before they are uploaded, so the cloud holds unreadable text.

Your choices

  • You may ask us to delete your account and its cloud data at any time. The copy on your own computer stays yours.
  • Deleting your shop from within the app removes its cloud records.

Questions about this policy, or want your data removed? Email adibhoye@gmail.com and we will respond within 7 working days.
